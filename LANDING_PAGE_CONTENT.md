## Section: Current Reality

Section label:

Current reality.

Headline:

Siloed data limits  
what AI can deliver.

Lead copy:

Critical workflows are spread across disconnected tools, with no shared structure, permissions, or history.

Supporting points:

AI sees fragments

Documents, spreadsheets, email threads, and legacy records do not add up to one trusted workflow boundary.

Private data moves too easily

Confidential context can be copied into external AI tools before access, storage, and approval rules are clear.

No accountable history

Teams need to know which source, human, model, and rule changed the state of work.

## Section: How Powerhouse Works

Section label:

How Powerhouse works.

Headline:

Map the workflow.  
Keep the boundary.

Lead copy:

Powerhouse turns your private processes into AI-ready software that you own. Explore solutions in a Powerhouse sandbox, then choose local, self-hosted, or remote deployment according to your needs.

Process steps:

1. Connect existing systems

Start from the documents, spreadsheets, inboxes, and legacy systems already carrying the work.

2. Structure the workflow

Model the objects, approvals, permissions, exceptions, and data history behind the process.

3. Add scoped AI assistance

Use AI to extract, compare, draft, flag, and route work inside the approved workflow boundary.

4. Keep human approval

Operators approve, reject, or revise important actions with the right context in front of them.

5. Retain attributable history

Every source, rule, model output, and human decision remains inspectable as the workflow evolves.

Control note:

Keep control of the workflow boundary.

Control note body:

We map your existing workflow into a Powerhouse sandbox. You retain control of access, storage, approvals, and operational history before a build starts.

## Section: Why Ownership Matters

Section label:

Why ownership matters.

Headline:

You own the boundary,  
the schema, and the history.

Body copy:

Powerhouse creates a controlled software boundary around the workflow. You decide who and what can access its data, where it is stored, which actions require approval, and how history is retained.

There is no rip-and-replace requirement. Powerhouse connects to existing systems and gives your team model choice as the workflow matures.

Ownership points:

Private deployment options

Choose local, self-hosted, or managed deployment according to the sensitivity and operational requirements of each workflow.

Open-source stack

Powerhouse is the open-source platform for owned operational software. Your team can inspect, extend, and operate the system.

Portable data and schemas

The useful asset is the structured workflow: data, schema, permissions, approvals, and history in formats you can keep.

Model choice

Scope assistance to the model and deployment path that fits the task, then change that choice as needs and policy evolve.

No rip-and-replace requirement

Powerhouse connects to existing systems and turns the valuable workflow into owned software over time.
