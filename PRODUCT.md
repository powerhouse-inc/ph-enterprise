# Powerhouse Enterprise Product Context

This file gives design and UI agents the product context they must load before
working on the public enterprise site.

## Surface

Public marketing site for Powerhouse Enterprise.

The primary route is `/` in `enterprise/site`. It explains the enterprise
proposition and routes qualified prospects to the BAI workflow assessment.

## Audience

Operations and technology leaders responsible for valuable, private,
document-heavy workflows that are spread across documents, spreadsheets,
inboxes, and legacy systems.

They are reading as serious buyers. They want to know what Powerhouse is, what
it lets them own, how AI is bounded, and what the first engagement produces.

## Positioning

Canonical promise:

> Turn workflows into software you own.

Homepage working headline:

> Turn workflows into software you own.

Powerhouse is the open-source platform for building owned operational software.
Powerhouse owns the first-workflow story. BAI is the engagement path for
delivery. Vetra is the tooling and deployment layer.

## Honest Claims

Allowed:

- owned operational software
- scoped AI assistance
- human approval
- attributable operation history
- private deployment options
- open-source stack
- portable data and schemas
- model choice
- no rip-and-replace starting path

Requires attribution or qualification:

- quantified savings
- security certifications
- regulatory compliance
- data residency
- SLA claims
- customer-specific production claims

## Evidence On Hand

- Real product screenshots in `enterprise/site/public/usecases`.
- BAI states 22 business modules are in active production.
- BAI attributes a 65% invoice-review time reduction to Wouter Vonk, CEO of
  Powerhouse.
- Production references include Powerhouse, BAI, Achra, Sky ecosystem work,
  Connect modules, and RFP Hub.

## Design Mode

The homepage should persuade, but it should do so like a technical operating
document rather than a generic SaaS landing page.

Mode: persuade with inspectable proof.

Voice:

- calm
- technical
- concrete
- ownership-oriented
- no hype

Anti-references:

- AI chatbot landing pages
- purple/cyan gradient SaaS templates
- glassmorphism dashboards
- hero metric soup
- industry buffet pages
- decorative SVG illustrations with no product evidence
